Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information: Name, email, phone number, role (student, teacher, parent, etc.), school information, profile data.

Educational Data: Course progress, grades, assignments, attendance records, learning analytics.

Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, usage patterns, camera access (security features).

2. How We Use Your Information

Educational Purposes: Deliver personalized learning experiences, track progress, provide analytics.

Communication: Facilitate parent-teacher communication, send notifications, updates.

Security: Monitor campus safety, manage access control, incident reporting.

Analytics: Improve platform performance, develop new features, research educational outcomes.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Firebase Authentication with secure token management
  • Data encryption in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Secure camera integration protocols

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Limited sharing occurs with:

  • Educational institutions for academic purposes
  • Parents/guardians (for student data)
  • Legal requirements (court orders, law enforcement)
  • Service providers (Firebase, Netlify) under strict contracts

5. Children's Privacy

We comply with COPPA and GDPR-K requirements. Student data is protected with additional safeguards and requires parental consent for users under 13.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request data deletion
  • Export your data
  • Opt-out of communications

7. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for educational purposes or as required by law. Student records are maintained according to educational retention policies.

8. International Data Transfers

Data is processed primarily in South Africa with some services provided through global providers like Firebase (Google Cloud). We ensure adequate protection through standard contractual clauses.
